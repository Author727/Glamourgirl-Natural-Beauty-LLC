    Our Story

    Glamourgirl Natural Beauty LLC was created for people who want their locs to look good and feel even better. Based in Snellville, Georgia, we focus completely on loc health, from the scalp to the tips.

    Every visit begins with a conversation. We learn about your hair, your routine and your goals, then build a service plan that fits you, whether your locs are ear-length or shoulder-length.

    From detox and deep conditioning to re-attachment and interlocking, our work is gentle, detailed and always done with your natural beauty in mind.

    Our Promise

    We believe healthy locs begin with a healthy scalp. That is why every service is gentle, unhurried and explained in plain language, so you always know what we are doing and why.

    We use hair-friendly techniques and residue-free products, and we never push services you do not need. Your comfort, your hair health and your trust come first.
